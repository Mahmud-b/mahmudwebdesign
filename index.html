Common questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need — a simple landing page costs less than a full multi-page business website. Send me a message with details about your business and I'll give you a clear quote.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the complexity of the site and how quickly you can share content like your logo, photos, and business details. I'll give you a realistic timeline once we've discussed your project.

Do I need to buy hosting and a domain?

Yes, every website needs a domain (your web address) and hosting (where the site lives online). I can guide you through getting both, or handle the setup for you.

Can I update my website myself?

Depending on how your site is built, you can either make simple updates yourself or send changes to me to handle. We'll agree on what works best for you before the project starts.

Do you build websites for businesses outside Nigeria?

My focus is on Nigerian businesses, but reach out and tell me about your project — if it's a good fit, we can work together.
